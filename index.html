Calculation rules

Advertisement: +3 calendar days; Pre-bid: +7 calendar days; Deadline: +12 calendar days. These move forward to the next working day when necessary. Clarification and supplemental bulletin dates are set 9 and 7 calendar days before the deadline and move back to the preceding working day. Subsequent activities use working days. Additional days are optional offsets to each activity's existing interval.
